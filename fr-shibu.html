As his parish describes him

From the profile written by St. Gregorios Orthodox Church, Bensalem — their own account of his ministry, not part of the official bio-data

Pastoral care

Marital enrichment, premarital guidance and grief counselling across the North East American Diocese; a campus counsellor to students from Chicago to Philadelphia; and support groups gathered for the widowed, the grieving, and those carrying mental illness.

Mission to the poor

A parish Mission Board feeding the homeless and serving Bucks and Philadelphia counties with food, clothing, diaper and school drives, and rehabilitation for refugees. Beyond America: meals packed for hungry nations, Operation Christmas Child, and foster education in India through Deepalaya.

Quietly, for years

More than ten houses built for the poor, and for over eighteen years the uplifting of more than fifty children through education, marriage and shelter. His stated hope is simple: to educate at least five children in every country, and to do mission alongside them.

The Retreat Center

The Holy Transfiguration Retreat Center at Dalton, which he directs, is described as 315 acres with 110,000 square feet of facilities, hosting thirty to thirty-five retreats a year, with a Community Center established in 2025 for mission across Eastern Pennsylvania.

Further study

Alongside the degrees listed above, the parish profile describes further study in youth ministry at Princeton Theological Seminary and in counselling and psychology. This does not appear among the qualifications in the published bio-data, so it is recorded here as the parish states it.

Bridges beyond the parish

Since 2016, through Building a Better Bensalem, building bridges of prayer among neighbours of every faith while guarding the younger generation from substance abuse.

Writing

Spiritual and theological articles contributed to various Church publications.
